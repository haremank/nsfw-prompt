# Scenes & themes

Scene sets the story; pick one scene + optional theme. Sensory anchors are prose-dialect only (skip for tag dialects).

## Scene quality coupling

Phone scenes (`selfie`, `mirror selfie`): casual clutter allowed, no studio words.
CCTV/door-crack: surveillance grain + timestamp, never cinematic grading.
Onsen/bath: mist volumetrics, wet specular skin.
Nightclub: halation, colored gels on skin.
